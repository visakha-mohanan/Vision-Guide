👁️ Interactive Eye Exercises

1. 20-20-20 Rule

Every 20 minutes, look at something 20 feet away for 20 seconds to relax eye muscles.

20

2. Palming

Relax tired eyes by cupping warm palms over them for a minute.

60

3. Near and Far Focus

Focus on your thumb, then shift gaze to an object 20+ feet away. Repeat for 2 minutes.

120

4. Figure Eight Tracking

Trace a large horizontal figure eight with your eyes slowly for 1–2 minutes.

60

5. Pencil Push-Ups

Hold a pencil at arm’s length and bring it toward your nose while focusing on the tip.

60

6. Eye Rolling

Slowly roll your eyes clockwise and then counter-clockwise for 30 seconds to relax eye muscles.

30
